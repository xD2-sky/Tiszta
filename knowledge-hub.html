Knowledge Hub

Technical knowledge for water, energy and environmental engineering.

Coming soon

A structured technical authority

The Knowledge Hub will be a searchable technical knowledge base positioning Tiszta Víz as a subject-matter authority across water, wastewater, desalination and environmental engineering. It will launch with the following sections:

01

Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) Technologies

Process explainers across ASP, SBR, MBBR, MBR and more, with pros, cons and applicable standards.

02

CPHEEO Manuals & Indian Standards

Plain-language summaries of official CPHEEO manuals and relevant IS codes, with links to source documents.

03

Water Treatment Plant (WTP) Technologies

Coverage of conventional treatment trains through to RO, UF, NF and advanced oxidation.

04

Desalination Technologies

Thermal and membrane desalination methods, including brine management and ZLD integration.

05

Water Reuse & Recycling

Non-potable reuse, indirect and direct potable reuse, and greywater recycling systems.

06

Sludge Management & Reuse

Every stage from dewatering through to biogas recovery, composting and resource recovery.

07

AWWA Standards Knowledge Base

Original summaries of AWWA standards indexed by category, linked to official sources.

08

Global Contracts Knowledge Base

FIDIC, EPC, DBO and PPP contract frameworks relevant to water infrastructure delivery.

09

Global Data Centre Water Requirements

Country-by-country research on data centre capacity and water demand.

This section is under active development and will be published progressively, following our phased website rollout.
