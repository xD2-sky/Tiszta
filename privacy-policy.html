Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Tiszta Víz Projects & Infrastructure Pvt. Ltd. ("Tiszta Víz", "we", "us", "our") operates this website. This policy explains what information we collect when you use it, why we collect it, and how it is handled.

Information We Collect

We only collect information you choose to submit through the forms on this site:

01

Contact form

Name, company, phone number, email address, project type, subject, message, and an optional RFP/tender document you choose to attach.

02

Careers form

Name, email address, phone number, area of interest, a short message about your background, and an optional resume/CV you choose to attach.

03

Newsletter sign-up

Your email address only.

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect payment information on this website.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit only to: respond to your enquiry or application; evaluate project, career, or partnership requests; and, if you opt in, send occasional updates about Tiszta Víz projects and technical articles. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Third-Party Service Providers

Form submissions on this website are processed by Web3Forms, a third-party form-delivery service, which securely transmits your submission to our email inbox. Web3Forms does not use your data for any purpose beyond this delivery. We recommend reviewing Web3Forms' own privacy policy for details of how they handle data in transit.

Cookies & Analytics

This website does not currently use cookies or analytics/advertising tracking scripts. If we add tools such as Google Analytics, Google Ads conversion tracking, or Meta Pixel in the future to understand site traffic and measure advertising performance, we will update this policy to describe what is collected and how to opt out, before those tools go live.

Data Retention

We retain submitted information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry, maintain business records, or comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information you submit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you've submitted to us at any time by contacting us using the details below.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and professional use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.
