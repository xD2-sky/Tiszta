Chapter 01 · Company profile

Who We Are

Tiszta Víz delivers sustainable engineering solutions across Water, Energy, and Infrastructure.

Tiszta Víz was founded on a simple premise: that water, energy, and infrastructure projects deliver best when engineered as one integrated system, not three separate contracts. That premise still shapes how we scope every project today.

As a new venture, our focus from day one is engineering discipline: pairing proven process technologies with dependable local delivery, so that every project stands on sound fundamentals rather than promises.
