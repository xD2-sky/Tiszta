Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) Technologies

Knowledge Hub / Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) Technologies

Sewage Treatment Plant (STP) technologies convert raw sewage into treated effluent suitable for safe discharge or reuse. The right technology depends on flow, land availability, effluent quality targets, and operating budget.
